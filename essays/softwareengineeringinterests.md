## Engineering To Protect My Future

As I continue to explore my path in software and computer engineering, one of the main areas of technology that strikes me is artificial intelligence. Furthermore, I am interested in understanding not only what AI is capable of, but also how engineers can create guardrails and limitations around it. As time goes on, the rapid rate at which AI is essentially taking over our every day lives is definitely alarming. This has sparked my interest in learning how this tool could be used responsibly without throwing security or harming our environment anymore. 

## Connecting the Network 

Wifi, connectivity, router, IP address, you name it. All of these terms have always sparked an interest in me throughout my engineering journey. As I further dive into what it means to be a software engineer, I want to better understand how programs interact with hardware and how networks are designed to maintain its reliability and safety. Because I am an aspiring computer engineer, being able to see how exactly the hardware and software of any system merge is very important to me. 

## The Future Ahead 

Down the road, I hope to begin my career in the Department of Defense. Over the past two summers, I have had the opportunity to experience two different companies within the DOD, and see how work in an office chair can directly support the warfighter or our people. More than anything, I would like a position where I can help other people, and ensure that I create tools or resources to assist whatever needs be. As I wrap up my final year of college, I would like to surround myself with experienced engineers in order to learn how they approach problems, and understand the decisions they made to shape their careers. I hope their experiences can help me grow into not only a better engineer, but someone who works well with others and is willing to continue to learn throughout my career.
