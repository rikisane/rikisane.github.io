As an intern at the Shipyard Infrastructure Optimization Program (SIOP PMO 555), I had the pleasure of improving the efficiency of data processing and analysis for a multi-million dollar project. A lot of my work revolved around finding ways to automate repetitive tasks, which overall saves a lot of time not only for SIOP, but also gets ships out to their fleet faster. 

One of the main projects that I worked on was developing Excel VBA macros, utilizing Power Query workflows to process more than 1,000 pages of PDF and Word documents. These tools extracted tabular data, and converted them into editable spreadsheets. As a result, documents were able to be reviewed a lot more efficiently and accurate version control was able to be maintained. 

I also helped to design an AI-based tool that converted handwritten notes and meeting minutes into structured summaries for internal use. Through these projects, I gained experience with automation and data processing, as well as figuring out what problems people have and how to provide an efficient solution for them.
